APASA

ONE EVIDENCE WORKSPACE

A clearer view of agriculture.

APASA is an agriculture policy and statistics workspace for Rwanda. It brings policy indicators, published crop observations and reviewed comparisons into one place, with their sources and interpretation limits.

For national planners

Review national policy references and explore seasonal statistics without losing their original definitions.

For district officers

Inspect published district and crop observations, then use reviewed evidence to support planning and follow-up.

EVIDENCE WITH ITS CONTEXT

The limits matter as much as the values.

APASA keeps provenance and comparison limits beside the evidence, so an observation is read for what it can support.

  • Sources stay attached. Trace evidence to the reviewed report, table, workbook or source cell.
  • Periods stay separate. Seasonal observations are not presented as annual totals or policy progress.
  • Missing is not zero. Unavailable values, context-only evidence and unsupported comparisons remain clearly labelled.
