Scope and interpretation
  • No national or provincial aggregation, district ranking or target-progress score is calculated. No reviewed policy target/actual pairing is directly comparable.
  • Geographic names and parent IDs are taken from the published district dataset. SAS estimates’ exact boundary-vintage equivalence remains unconfirmed.
  • The display maps only the case-only label “Cooking Banana” to “Cooking banana” so its four measures appear together. Source details retain each original label. No other crop mapping is inferred.
  • These official district estimates are not farmer records, programme delivery logs, eligibility decisions or a list of farmers needing follow-up. No comparison with another season is made.
