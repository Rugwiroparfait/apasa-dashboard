PSTA5 · Official national evidence

National policymaker

For policymakers and programme analysts reviewing crop policy evidence.

Your task: inspect PSTA5 indicators, source-backed baselines and targets, official actuals and comparison limitations.

What it tells you: which policy references and official statistics are available, their reporting periods and where evidence gaps remain.

Limit: PSTA5 target progress is not calculated because no reviewed target/actual pairing is directly comparable. Context-only actuals are labelled.

NISR SAS · Published district statistics

District agriculture officer

For district officers and agricultural analysts reviewing official crop statistics for their area.

Your task: select a province or City of Kigali, then a district and crop, and inspect the published figures and source cells.

What it tells you: cultivated area, harvested area, average crop yield and production from NISR SAS 2026 Season B (March–June 2026), covering 30 districts and 15 crop columns.

Limit: these one-season survey estimates do not show programme delivery, farmer eligibility or district target attainment.

Separate survey research

AHS 2024 Section 3_4 · Descriptive sample records

AHS maize research evidence

For researchers and analysts exploring the published maize sample findings.

Your task: review reported harvest quantities in kilograms, farming-practice comparisons, missing reports, reported zeros and the large-quantity sensitivity summary.

What it tells you: what the analyzed AHS crop-slot records report, including descriptive associations.

Limit: AHS 2024 results describe survey records and are not official district estimates or national production. They do not establish causal effects or crop-specific yield.

Keep the sources separate

Policy targets, published NISR district estimates and AHS sample descriptions answer different questions. Their units, periods and populations must be checked before making comparisons. Missing evidence means unavailable in the reviewed sources, not zero.

This public, read-only workspace does not provide programme-delivery records or individual farmer information.
