Methods & limitations

  • AHS 2024 Section 3_4 only. Quantities are kilograms per maize crop-slot/plot-season record, not household totals. Seasonal totals may include quantities remaining to be harvested.
  • Descriptive and unweighted. No national estimates, district scores or geographic filters. Several plots can belong to one household. Survey strata and PSU details remain unconfirmed.
  • No crop-specific yield or productivity is calculated: a verified crop-area denominator is unavailable. No confidence intervals or causal effects are reported.
  • Missing quantities are excluded from harvest statistics. Reported zeros are distinct; large quantities are excluded only in the labeled overall sensitivity view. No group-specific sensitivity summaries are exposed.
  • Plot practices may apply to other crops on mixed-crop plots. Small or sensitive groups are suppressed or omitted, including complementary suppression.
