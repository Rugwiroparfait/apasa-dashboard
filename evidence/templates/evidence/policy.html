Reading this evidence responsibly

These are policy references and official statistics. Seasonal actuals are context only: a matching crop name or unit does not establish a matching policy definition or reporting period. Target geography is retained as recorded; national strategy context does not establish district targets.

This page does not show whether programmes reached farmers or whether a district met a target. It provides no programme-delivery records, district rankings or recommendations. Missing evidence is not zero and does not mean no data exist elsewhere.

AHS crop-slot harvest descriptions are a separate source and are not used here. National figures are read directly from the reviewed policy evidence inventory; district values are never summed. Source strings and units are displayed without conversion.

The evidence inventory records baseline, 2025/26 and 2028/29 references only. Other target years and source anomalies outside those fields are not imported or repaired here.
