Data origin · Demonstration data

Synthetic / illustrative — not official statistics. These aggregate records were generated for APASA interface demonstration. They are not official NISR or MINAGRI statistics and must not be used for analysis or policy decisions.

Dataset: APASA interface demo v1 · Generation date: 31 October 2026. Values and targets are invented. Administrative names identify example locations only; no published statistical values were used to generate these examples.

District examples use deterministic cultivated area, 90% harvested area, and illustrative yield; production = harvested hectares × kilograms per hectare ÷ 1,000. Missing and true-zero examples are intentional. Planning examples use explicitly listed annual observations and invented targets. Periods remain separate. No sampling design, sample size, verification or official policy status is claimed.

Variables, units, formulas, input labels and generation commands are documented in data/demo/README.md and data/demo/manifest.json.
